StampCams Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

StampCams is a camera app that adds the date, time, location and project details to your photos. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything stays on your iPhone.

No account, no server, no tracking

StampCams has no sign-in and no server of its own. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code. Your photos, locations, notes, projects and templates are stored only on your iPhone. The developer never receives them.

Camera

The camera is used only to take the photos you capture. Photos are saved inside the app, and also to your Photos library if you turn on Save to Photos.

Location

Your location is used only while the camera is open, to put coordinates, altitude, heading and the address on your photos. The app does not use location in the background. If you don't allow location, photos are stamped with the time and your project details only.

Addresses from Apple

To show a street address, the app sends the photo's coordinates to Apple's address lookup service, which is part of iOS. That request is covered by Apple's privacy policy. No other information is sent anywhere.

Photos library

The app only asks to add photos to your library. It cannot see or read your existing photos.

Sharing

Photos leave the app only when you share or export them yourself, using the iOS share sheet.

Deleting your data

Deleting a photo or project in the app removes it from the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Copies you saved to Photos or shared stay where you put them.

Children

StampCams is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
